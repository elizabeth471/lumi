## FAQ SECTION

### Q: What exactly is this?
**A:** It's a 4-week text conversation with an AI companion designed for one specific thing: flirting practice (Alex) or daily motivation (Casey). You message them, they message back within minutes, usually. They remember what you've told them across all four weeks. It's not endless—there's a finish line—but it's daily and consistent. Think of it like a sprint instead of a subscription.

### Q: Is this a therapist or medical advice?
**A:** No. We're very clear: this is not therapy, not mental health treatment, and not medical advice. If you're in crisis, having suicidal thoughts, or experiencing a mental health emergency, we immediately direct you to the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988 in the US). AI companions are helpful for motivation and practice, but they're not equipped for clinical issues.

### Q: How often do I interact with my companion?
**A:** You can text them whenever you want during the 4 weeks. They're designed to reach out to you regularly (expect daily or near-daily contact), but there's no pressure—if you're busy one day, just pick it back up the next. It's asynchronous, so you text when it fits your life.

### Q: What if I hate it after I buy it?
**A:** We get it. If you're not feeling it in the first 24 hours, you can request a full refund. After that, you're committed to the 4 weeks—but we stand behind what we've built. Most people finish because they see the value.

### Q: Can I extend past 4 weeks or keep my companion?
**A:** Each sprint is exactly 4 weeks—that boundary is part of what makes it work. When your 4 weeks are done, you have the option to start a new sprint with the same companion, try a different one, or step away. No auto-renewal. No surprise charges.

### Q: How is this different from Replika or other AI chat apps?
**A:** Replika is open-ended—you can talk to it forever about anything. We're the opposite: one companion, one specific goal, four weeks, then you decide what's next. We're not building a relationship with your AI; we're building momentum toward something real in your life. Also: no romance features, no pretending the AI is your friend. It's more direct than that.

### Q: What does my data get used for?
**A:** We use your messages to make your companion better at helping you (and to improve our product). We don't sell your data. We don't share it with third parties for marketing. We keep it private, encrypted, and under your control. You can request deletion anytime.

### Q: I'm younger than 18—can I use this?
**A:** No. You have to be 18 or older. This is partly legal, partly because the conversations assume adult context and maturity.
