## Slide 2 — The Problem

**Parents are stuck between two bad options.**

→ **Give the smartphone:** Content risks. Social risks. Addiction risks. No visibility.

→ **Hold the line:** Kids feel left out. Social exclusion is real. Parents feel like the villain.

**There is no good third option.**

Not yet.

*Speaker note: Don't over-explain. Let it sit. Every parent in the room has lived this.*

---

## Slide 3 — Why Now

**The moment is here.**

- **Phone-free school movement** is growing fast — hundreds of schools, organized parent networks, national attention
- **Jonathan Haidt's research** (*The Anxious Generation*, 2024) gave parents mainstream permission to act
- **App stores are flooded** with "kids apps" — none are purpose-built devices for this age group and this concern
- **Parents are organizing** — and actively looking for alternatives to recommend to each other

The demand exists. The community exists. No credible product exists yet.

---

## Slide 4 — What Lumi Is

**The third option.**

A device and platform designed specifically for children aged 6–12 that:

- Satisfies the social and digital pressure kids face
- Keeps content safe and age-appropriate
- Brings families closer instead of pulling them apart
- Is built for multilingual, neurodivergent-aware households from day one

**Core belief:** The only screen time worth allowing is screen time that brings families closer.

---

## Slide 5 — Who It's For

**Wave 1 target: phone-free movement families**

- Parents of children aged 6–12
- Screen-time-conscious, actively looking for alternatives
- Multilingual households (Spanish, Portuguese, Filipino/Tagalog, French)
- Neurodivergent-aware parents who want structured, predictable digital environments
- Already organized in communities — word-of-mouth spreads fast here

**Why this group first:** They're not waiting to be convinced. They're already convinced. They want a product to point to.

---

## Slide 6 — The Product Vision

**What a child actually does with Lumi:**

- Supervised, structured video experiences (not a free YouTube scroll)
- Game-based learning with adaptive difficulty — meets the child where they are
- Multilingual content built in, not bolted on
- Parent dashboard: real visibility into what the child is doing and how they're developing
- Phone-free community features — connection without the social media risk

**What parents get:** A device they can hand over without anxiety.

**What kids get:** Something that actually feels like theirs.
