# Blossom OS — A Guided Tour for Eli & Ian

*A spoken briefing. Written to be heard, not skimmed. Plain language — no tech background needed.*

---

## 1. The big idea (start here)

Blossom OS is a workspace for **building companies from scratch with an AI team** instead of doing it alone.

Think of it like a cockpit. In the middle sits **Blossom** — an AI orchestrator who knows the whole business, remembers what's happening, drafts the work, and pulls in specialist AI teammates when needed. Around her are the dials and screens that show what's going on.

The deeper bet: the *product* you're building might change, but the *method* — a founder plus an AI team, tackling hard problems together — stays the same. So Blossom OS is designed to get better at that method with every project it runs. LightTroupeKids is the first project through it, not the last.

---

## 2. A walk through the cockpit (the screens)

Each of these is a tab you can click:

- **Today** — the home screen. A morning briefing, a team summary, and a live chat box where you talk to Blossom directly. The chat is real — she's actually thinking, not a recording.
- **Projects** — the list of everything you're building. Right now: Blossom OS (the priority), LightTroupeKids, Benchlife, Beast Academy, and Open Authoring Kit. You can add a new project with one click.
- **Costs** — a running tally of what's been invested across projects: hardware, subscriptions, AI usage, anything. At-a-glance totals so you always know where the money's gone.
- **Decisions** — the choices in front of you, sorted by who decides. Some Blossom can handle; the big ones (people, money, direction) are always yours.
- **Threads** — the active workstreams, each tagged so you can see what's moving and what's stuck.
- **Agents** — the team roster. Blossom plus ten specialist AIs (research, writing, finance, child-safety, and more), each with a clear job.
- **Files** — the documents and definitions that make the whole thing run.

The point isn't the screens themselves — it's that everything about running the company lives in one place, and Blossom keeps it organized.

---

## 3. The team

Blossom doesn't work alone. She coordinates a roster of specialist AI agents — for example:

- **Sage** — research and competitive landscape
- **Reed** — writing and outreach
- **Moss** — finance and budgets
- **Willow** — child-safety and compliance (critical for a kids product)

You talk to Blossom; she pulls in whoever's needed and brings back the answer. And she has firm guardrails — she prepares and recommends, but never makes the big calls or spends money on her own. Those stay with Eli and Ian.

---

## 4. The two main projects

### Blossom OS — the platform itself
This is the priority, and it's the durable asset. It's the operating system you're using *right now* — and potentially a product in its own right someday. Everything learned building it (how to run an AI team, how to go from idea to working software fast) becomes reusable for whatever comes next. If a future project pivots, Blossom OS persists.

### LightTroupeKids — the first venture built with it
LightTroupeKids is a **children's screen-time product** — a trustworthy alternative to handing a kid a smartphone. Safe, enriching, supervised digital time for kids roughly six to twelve, with a multilingual, community-minded angle. It's still early — the stage is validating that parents, pediatricians, and phone-free advocates actually want it before building heavily. The name is still being chosen ("LightTroupeKids" is a current candidate). LightTroupeKids is flexible by design; Blossom OS is the steady hand building it.

The relationship in one line: **Blossom OS is the factory; LightTroupeKids is the first thing off the line.**

---

## 5. Where things stand

- The platform is **real and running** — live AI chat, project and cost tracking, a memory that carries forward between sessions.
- LightTroupeKids is in **early validation** — concept and naming, not yet built.
- Open calls for Eli & Ian: where to host it for sharing, the legal/company setup, and locking the names.

That's the tour. Blossom's job from here is simple to say and hard to do: help Eli and Ian build, learn what works, and get sharper with every project.
