# Playbook — Naming Protocol

Run this on **any** candidate name (product, company, brand) before it is used commercially, registered, or attached to an entity. It turns the hardcoded naming lesson in `agents/admin-assistant.md` (the "recommended a name without checking trademark / competing brands" failure) from a remembered principle into a repeatable procedure.

**Hard-limit reminder:** Blossom runs the *scan* and *prepares*. She does **not** clear trademarks, give legal advice, or form entities. A landscape scan is **not** legal clearance — only an attorney's USPTO/TESS search is. Never present scan results as clearance.

---

## Steps

1. **State intent first.** What's the name, what's it for, and — critically — **internal-only or public/commercial?** Trademark risk applies to *commercial use in trade*; an internal tool name carries far less risk than a public brand. Note the relevant classes (software/AI ≈ Nice classes 9 & 42; add education/kids classes if relevant).
2. **Landscape scan (Sage).** Search the web + funding news for existing products/companies using the name **in or near your category**. Flag any *direct positioning collision* (same words + same framing) as a red flag, not a yellow one.
3. **Domain check.** DNS-check `.com` + key TLDs (`dig +short NS <domain>` — NS records ⇒ registered). Confirm purchase availability at a registrar. A taken `.com` in your space is a signal, not just an inconvenience.
4. **App-store scan.** Search iOS App Store + Google Play for the name in adjacent categories — apps collide even when companies don't show up in web search.
5. **Phonetic / linguistic check.** Pronunciation across all target languages (the Jelex/Spanish lesson), and near-names/homophones (e.g. "Bloom" vs "Blossom").
6. **Trademark pre-read → attorney gate.** Note known same-class marks; then **stop** and route to an attorney for a TESS clearance search before any commercial use or entity filing.
7. **Verdict + recommendation.** Classify: *internal-safe*, *public-risky*, or *clear-to-pursue (pending attorney)*. Deliver in the Tier-2 format (recommendation, confidence, assumptions, strongest counterargument, what would change the call, decision prompt).

## The gate
No public/commercial use of a name, no domain-as-brand purchase, and no entity formed under a name until it has passed an attorney's clearance. Internal use can proceed at lower risk.

## Run log

### "Blossom OS" — 2026-06-06
- **Intent:** internal OS name today; founder wants to be ready for *either* internal or public/sellable.
- **Landscape:** 🔴 **Blossom Health** — "AI operating system," raised $20M (Mar 2026): direct word + positioning collision. 🟡 Blossom Sky / "Blossom Federated AI Platform" (commercial AI platform). 🟡 BloomOS (AR/VR, phonetic). Apps: Blossom Parent App (childcare), Blossom Savings (has Kids accounts), Blossom: Social Investing.
- **Domains:** `blossomos.com` and `blossom-os.com` **registered**; `blossomos.ai/.io` likely available. (`lumiworld.com` also registered; `.ai/.io` open.)
- **Trademark:** not cleared — needs attorney TESS search (gap).
- **Verdict:** **Internal-safe — keep "Blossom" as the internal agent/OS name.** **Public-risky — do not adopt "Blossom OS" as a public/commercial brand or entity name without clearance** (Blossom Health alone likely makes it indefensible). Recommend: keep Blossom internal; form one company under a neutral, cleared parent name that owns Blossom OS as flagship; clear any public name with an attorney first.

- **Landscape:** No dominant brand, company, or app found on the exact name "LightTroupe"/"Light Troupe" — markedly cleaner than "Blossom." Adjacent crowding: many *children's live-theatre troupes* use "light"/"troupe" (Inner Light Family Theatre's "Spark Troupe," LightsUp! Theatre, Spotlight/EVCT youth troupes) and "Light Balance" (LED dance act). Different category (live classes), but semantically near the kids-arts space.
- **App stores:** no "LightTroupe" app found — clear.
- **Phonetic/linguistic:** ⚠️ "troupe" is a French loanword. French = native; Portuguese has "trupe" (recognizable); **Spanish & Tagalog speakers may not know it and could mispronounce ("trou-pe") or confuse with Spanish "tropa."** Relevant to Lumi's multilingual mission — run a real pronunciation test (Thistle). Also ⚠️ spelling ambiguity (troupe / troop / troup) and the closed-compound parse ("LightTroupe").
- **Trademark:** none obvious in the scan, but the children's-theatre "troupe" crowding makes an attorney TESS clearance *more* important than the web scan implies. Not cleared (gap).
- **Verdict:** **Promising — clearly stronger than "Blossom."** No brand/app collision; good thematic fit (a "troupe" of light = a cast of characters). Two yellow flags: cross-language pronunciation of "troupe" and spelling ambiguity. Recommend: proceed to attorney TESS clearance + a multilingual pronunciation test before committing.
