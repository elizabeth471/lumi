Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an activity schedule? +
Go to the Activity Library, select an activity, and click "Assign to Child." You can set specific days and times when the activity will be available.
What happens when the daily limit is reached? +
The activity will pause and your child will see a friendly message. As a parent, you receive a notification and can adjust the limit from your dashboard.
Is lighttroupekids safe for my child? +
Yes. Every activity is personally reviewed. We comply with COPPA, never track personal data, and parents control all settings. See our Safety page for details.
Can I see what my child is doing? +
Yes. You get weekly summaries, reflections from your child, and detailed progress reports. You control all visibility and notification settings.
