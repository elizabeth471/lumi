# Income Workflow Experiment

**Purpose:** Generate $1K-$5K+/month passive revenue to fund Lumi development and AI infrastructure while Lumi validates.

**Operational Model:**
- Zero founder customer interaction (no calls, no emails, no support)
- Multiple simultaneous revenue streams (Fiverr + App Store + digital products)
- AI-managed delivery and automation
- Founder involvement: Setup + 2-3 light tasks/week (upload, post, check)

**Status:** In Planning Phase

---

## Project Goals

- **Month 1:** Launch 3-5 initial products, generate $200-$500
- **Month 3:** Expand to 8-10 products, generate $1K-$2K/month
- **Month 6:** Optimized portfolio, generate $2K-$5K+/month

---

## Revenue Streams (Planned)

1. **Fiverr Gigs** — Automated delivery gigs (templates, voice-based, etc.)
2. **App Store** — Subscription/paid apps (friend apps, games, utilities)
3. **Digital Products** — Gumroad/Lemonsqueezy (guides, templates, prompts)
4. **Voice-Based Products** — AI voice apps, personalized voice services
5. **Browser Games/Interactive Experiences** — Itch.io, web-based

---

## Key Constraints

- **Founder time:** Max 2-3 hours/week (setup + monitoring)
- **No customer interaction:** All delivery automated or handled by AI
- **Multiple streams:** Blossom manages portfolio across platforms
- **Voice available:** Elizabeth can record voice samples for AI use
