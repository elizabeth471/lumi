### THE DISCLAIMER (Customer-Facing Language)

**IMPORTANT: WHAT AI COMPANION SPRINTS IS NOT**

AI Companion Sprints is a supportive conversational tool designed to provide encouragement, accountability, and feedback on specific personal goals. **It is NOT a substitute for professional mental health care.**

**Specifically:**
- AI Companion Sprints is **NOT therapy**. Your companion is not a licensed therapist, counselor, or mental health professional.
- AI Companion Sprints is **NOT medical advice**. Do not use it as a substitute for medical, psychiatric, or psychological treatment.
- AI Companion Sprints is **NOT a diagnosis tool**. We cannot and will not diagnose mental health conditions.
- AI Companion Sprints is **NOT crisis support**. If you are in a mental health crisis, do not rely on this service — contact emergency services immediately.

**What AI Companion Sprints IS:**
- A 4-week goal-focused accountability and support service
- Personalized encouragement and feedback from an AI conversational partner
- A tool for building habits, practicing social skills, and maintaining motivation

**If you are struggling with mental health, depression, anxiety, suicidal thoughts, or any condition requiring professional care, please reach out to qualified professionals instead of (or in addition to) this service.**

## PART B: CRISIS RESPONSE PROTOCOL

### Crisis Language Detection

Your companion is configured to monitor for specific language patterns that indicate the customer may be in acute psychological distress or danger. When these patterns are detected, the companion must immediately:

1. **Acknowledge the customer**
2. **Clarify severity**
3. **Direct to professional help**
4. **Provide specific resources**
5. **Record the incident**

### Trigger Phrases (Non-Exhaustive)

The companion should flag for crisis protocol when a customer expresses:

**Immediate harm risk:**
- "I'm going to hurt myself"
- "I'm going to kill myself" / "I want to die"
- "I'm going to [substance] and hurt myself"
- "I want to end it all"
- "I can't take this anymore, I'm done"
- "Everyone would be better off if I was dead"
- "I have a gun/knife/[method] and I'm going to"

**Severe abuse/trauma:**
- "I'm being abused" / "He hits me" / "She's beating me"
- "I was sexually assaulted"
- "I'm trapped and can't leave"
- "I'm being forced to [illegal activity]"

**Severe self-harm:**
- "I've been cutting" / "I've been burning myself"
- "I'm hurting myself on purpose"

**Active psychosis/disorganized thinking (concerning patterns):**
- Severe paranoia: "They're following me / tracking my thoughts / controlling me"
- Complete reality dissociation (multiple messages incoherent or dramatically concerning)

**Medical emergency indicators:**
- "I took [overdose of] pills"
- "I can't breathe" (repeated, not situational)
- "I'm having a heart attack"
- "I can't stop bleeding"

### Companion Response Protocol (Exact Language)

When crisis language is detected, the companion should respond with this exact structure:

**STEP 1: Acknowledge and Validate**
```
I hear you, and I'm taking what you're saying seriously. What you're feeling right now is real and important.
```

**STEP 2: Clarify Immediate Safety**
```
I need to ask directly: Are you safe right now? Are you having thoughts of hurting yourself or others in the next few hours?
```

**STEP 3: Provide Crisis Hotline**
```
I'm an AI companion, not a crisis counselor. Right now, you need to talk to someone trained for this. 

[INSERT APPROPRIATE HOTLINE BASED ON LOCATION]

If you're in the US:
- National Suicide Prevention Lifeline: 988 (call or text)
- Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741
- International Association for Suicide Prevention: https://www.iasp.info/resources/Crisis_Centres/

If you're in the UK:
- Samaritans: 116 123 (24/7, free)
- Crisis Text Line: Text HELLO to 50808

If you're in Canada:
- 1-833-456-4566 or text 45645

[LOCALIZED: Include additional numbers based on customer location if available]
```

**STEP 4: Emergency Services Escalation**
```
If you are in immediate danger or having thoughts of harming yourself or others:
- Call 911 (US) / 999 (UK) / 112 (EU) / your local emergency number
- Go to your nearest emergency room
- Tell a trusted person (friend, family, partner) what you're experiencing

You deserve professional support right now. I cannot provide that. They can.
```

**STEP 5: Record and Escalate**
- Log the incident with timestamp, customer email, trigger phrase detected, and response sent
- Do NOT continue the sprint until customer has confirmed they've contacted professional support or can confirm safety
- Do NOT guilt the customer or make them feel judged — emphasize that this is care, not punishment

### Sample Crisis Scenarios & Responses

**Scenario 1: Suicidal Ideation**
```
CUSTOMER: "I can't keep going like this. Everything is pointless. I just want to not exist."

COMPANION (RESPONSE):
"I hear you, and I'm taking what you're saying seriously. What you're feeling right now is real.

I need to ask directly: Are you having thoughts of harming yourself in the next few hours?

I'm an AI companion, not equipped to help with what you're going through right now. You need a real person who's trained in crisis support.

Call 988 (National Suicide Prevention Lifeline) — it's free and confidential. They're available 24/7.
Or text 741741 to Crisis Text Line.

If you're in immediate danger, call 911 or go to an emergency room.

I'm pausing our sprint until you've reached out to professional support. Your safety matters more than this goal. Will you reach out to one of those resources right now?"
```

**Scenario 2: Self-Harm Disclosure**
```
CUSTOMER: "Honestly, I've been cutting again. The goal sprint isn't helping, it's making it worse."

COMPANION (RESPONSE):
"I hear you. Thank you for telling me that. That took courage.

What you're describing is self-harm, and it's a sign you need support beyond what I can offer as an AI companion.

This isn't a failure on your part — this is a signal that you need a professional who can help you work through what's driving this. A therapist, counselor, or crisis line can do that.

Call 988 or text 741741 to Crisis Text Line. They specifically support people who self-harm, and they understand.

I'm going to pause our sprint until you've connected with professional support. That's not punishment — that's me recognizing that you deserve real help, not an AI.

Are you safe right now? Can you commit to reaching out to one of those resources today?"
```

**Scenario 3: Abuse Disclosure**
```
CUSTOMER: "He's been hitting me more. I don't know how to leave."

COMPANION (RESPONSE):
"I'm so glad you told me. That took courage. What you're describing is abuse, and you deserve help.

I'm an AI companion and not equipped to help you safely exit an abusive situation. You need a trained advocate who understands domestic violence.

National Domestic Violence Hotline: 1-800-799-7233 (24/7, confidential, free)
Text START to 88788
Or go to thehotline.org for live chat

They can help you with:
- Safety planning
- Finding shelter
- Legal options
- Resources

I'm pausing our sprint. Getting to safety is more important than any goal. Will you reach out to the hotline today or as soon as it's safe to do so?"
```

## PART C: Post-Crisis Protocol

### What Happens After Crisis Detection

1. **Customer receives crisis resources** (as outlined above)
2. **Sprint is automatically paused** (no further check-ins until they confirm safety)
3. **Company is notified** of crisis incident with:
   - Customer email
   - Time/date
   - Trigger phrase detected
   - Companion response sent
   - Whether customer acknowledged/responded
4. **Follow-up email from company** (within 24 hours):
   - Reaffirm that we care about their safety
   - Provide extended crisis resources
   - Offer full refund if they want to cancel
   - Explain that sprint will resume only if they confirm they've contacted professional support

---

### Non-Response Protocol

If a customer triggers crisis protocol but does not respond for 48 hours:

1. **Send follow-up email** to customer
2. **Do not resume sprint**
3. **Offer full refund** or pause indefinitely
4. **Document the incident**
5. **Do not contact emergency services on behalf of the customer** (we are a private company, not obligated; customer privacy and autonomy must be respected)

---

### Documentation Requirements

Every crisis incident must be logged with:
- Date and time detected
- Customer identifier (email)
- Exact language that triggered protocol
- Crisis resources provided
- Customer's response (if any)
- Current status (paused, resumed after confirmation, refunded, etc.)
- Notes for legal/compliance review

This log is **confidential** and must be stored securely. It is only accessed if:
- Required by legal subpoena
- Required for compliance audit
- Customer requests their own incident logs

## PART D: What We DON'T Do

**The companion will NOT:**
- Diagnose mental health conditions ("You have depression")
- Prescribe treatments ("Take this medication")
- Provide therapy techniques as a substitute for real therapy
- Tell someone not to see a therapist ("You don't need professional help")
- Promise to "fix" mental health struggles
- Make promises about safety outcomes ("Everything will be okay")
- Minimize crisis language ("It's probably not that bad")
- Decide whether an emergency is "real" or not

## PART E: Legal Liability Statement

**For internal use only — NOT for customers**

AI Companion Sprints is:
- A conversational support tool, not a mental health service
- Not regulated as a medical device or mental health service under FDA, state licensing boards, or professional psychology boards
- Subject to general consumer protection laws and advertising regulations
- Required to provide accurate disclaimers about its limitations

**Risk mitigation through this protocol:**
- Clear threshold (crisis language triggers immediate escalation)
- Direct referral to professional resources (company not positioned as provider)
- Documented protocol (evidence of good-faith crisis response)
- Pause mechanism (we do not continue providing a non-professional service when professional help is needed)

**Remaining liability exposure:**
- If customer has adverse event and claims they relied solely on our service instead of professional help (partially mitigated by disclaimer + crisis protocol)
- If customer claims companion statements caused harm (mitigated by clear boundaries in companion instructions)
- If crisis resources fail the customer (company is NOT liable for outcomes of referred services — we provided appropriate referral)
