## 3. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

### 3.1 Primary Purposes

**To Provide the Service:**
- Creating and managing your account
- Delivering companion messages and check-ins
- Processing payments and refunds
- Storing your conversation history for you to review
- Responding to customer support requests

**To Improve the Service:**
- Understanding how customers use the Service
- Identifying bugs and technical issues
- Analyzing companion conversation effectiveness
- Improving companion personality and responses
- Testing new features

**Legal & Safety:**
- Complying with applicable laws
- Enforcing our Terms of Service
- Detecting and preventing fraud
- Responding to law enforcement requests
- Protecting our users and company from harm

### 3.2 Machine Learning & Model Training

**Our Approach:**
- We use anonymized conversation data to improve Claude (our AI model)
- This means: conversation content is stripped of identifying information before it's used for training
- You can opt-out of this (see Section 7)

**Specifically:**
- Raw conversation text with your name removed = "anonymized"
- Timestamps, user IDs, email addresses = removed before training use
- The trained model is NOT shared; it improves our internal service only

**What We DO NOT do:**
- Share your conversations with third parties
- Use your data to train a model we sell to competitors
- Identify you in any public use of your data
- Use conversation data for marketing purposes

### 3.3 Communication & Marketing

**Transactional Emails:**
- Confirmation of purchase
- Companion first message
- Sprint reminder emails
- Refund confirmations
- Support responses

These are necessary for the Service and you cannot opt out.

**Optional Marketing Emails:**
- Updates about new companion types
- Promotional offers or discounts
- Product news or feature announcements

You can opt out of marketing emails at any time by clicking "Unsubscribe" in the email.

**We will NOT:**
- Share your email with third parties for marketing
- Send marketing emails without your consent
- Continue marketing emails after you've asked to stop

## 4. DATA SHARING & THIRD PARTIES

### 4.1 Who We Share Data With

**Anthropic (Claude API Provider):**
- We send your conversation to Anthropic's Claude API to generate companion responses
- Anthropic's API has its own data use terms (see anthropic.com/privacy)
- We only send what's necessary for the companion to respond
- You can review Anthropic's terms before using our Service

**Stripe (Payment Processor):**
- We share your name, email, and billing address with Stripe to process payments
- Stripe does NOT see your conversation data
- Stripe has its own privacy policy (stripe.com/privacy)

**Google Analytics:**
- We use Google Analytics to understand how customers use our website
- Google collects anonymized usage data (page views, button clicks, etc.)
- Google does NOT see your conversation data or personal identity
- You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking in your browser settings

**Service Providers:**
- Email delivery (email service provider)
- Hosting (cloud infrastructure provider)
- Customer support tools
- Analytics and monitoring

All service providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only for providing services to us.

### 4.2 We Do NOT Share Data With

We explicitly do NOT:
- Sell your data to other companies
- Share your data with advertisers or marketers
- Share conversation data with third parties (except Anthropic for API processing)
- Share data with law enforcement unless legally required

### 4.3 Law Enforcement & Legal Requests

If we receive a legal request (subpoena, warrant, court order) for your data:
- We will comply with applicable law
- We will provide only the specific data requested
- We will notify you (unless legally prohibited from doing so)
- We will challenge overbroad requests

**We cannot guarantee that law enforcement will not subpoena your data — but we will not voluntarily share it without a legal requirement.**

## 5. DATA STORAGE & SECURITY

### 5.1 Where Your Data Is Stored

**Primary Storage:**
- Google Cloud Platform (US-based data centers)
- Backups in multiple geographic locations

**Conversation Data:**
- Stored in encrypted databases
- Accessible only to our authorized personnel and customer support

**Payment Data:**
- NOT stored by us (Stripe stores and secures this)
- We only store the last 4 digits of your card for reference

### 5.2 Security Measures

We use:
- SSL/TLS encryption for all data in transit (HTTPS)
- AES-256 encryption for data at rest
- Secure password hashing (bcrypt or equivalent)
- Regular security audits and penetration testing
- Access controls (employees only access data needed for their role)
- Monitoring for unauthorized access attempts

**We do NOT claim 100% security.** No system is perfectly secure, but we implement industry-standard protections.

### 5.3 Data Breaches

If we discover a breach affecting your personal information:
- We will notify you within 30 days
- We will explain what data was compromised
- We will provide guidance on protecting yourself
- We will comply with applicable breach notification laws

## 6. DATA RETENTION & DELETION

### 6.1 How Long We Keep Your Data

**While Your Account Is Active:**
- Your personal information is kept as long as needed to provide the Service
- Your conversation history is kept for the duration of your sprint
- After your sprint ends, conversation history is retained for 90 days (in case you want to review)

**If You Cancel Your Account:**
- Personal information (name, email, goal) is deleted within 30 days
- Conversation history is deleted within 30 days
- Payment records are retained for 7 years (tax/accounting purposes) but separated from your identity

**Anonymized Data:**
- Aggregated, anonymized data may be retained indefinitely for:
  - Improving our Service
  - Analytics and research
  - Model training (see Section 3.2)

This data cannot identify you.

### 6.2 Right to Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your data:
- Email support@aisprintscompany.com and request "data deletion"
- We will delete your personal information within 30 days
- We will send you confirmation when deletion is complete

**Limitations:**
- We must retain some data for tax/legal purposes (see 6.1)
- If data is already anonymized, we cannot delete it (it's not identifiable)
- After deletion, we cannot recover your account or conversation history

## 8. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY (COPPA)

### 8.1 Age Requirement

AI Companion Sprints is designed for users 18 years and older. We do NOT knowingly collect data from children under 13.

**If you are under 18:**
- Do not use this Service
- Do not create an account
- Do not purchase a sprint

**If we discover we've collected data from someone under 13:**
- We will delete it immediately
- We will notify the parent/guardian
- We will comply with COPPA requirements

### 8.2 Parental Rights

If you are a parent and believe we've collected data from your child:
- Email support@aisprintscompany.com
- Provide your child's name and email
- We will delete the data within 30 days

### 8.3 Why the 18+ Requirement?

- The Service involves emotional support and goal-setting (risks for younger users)
- Some customers may disclose sensitive personal information
- Mental health content could be harmful to younger users without parental involvement
- We are NOT equipped to provide child-safe interaction

If we expand to serve younger users in the future, we will:
- Obtain parental consent
- Implement child-specific privacy protections
- Comply fully with COPPA
- Consult with child safety experts

## 9. INTERNATIONAL USERS (GDPR & CCPA)

### 9.1 European Users (GDPR)

If you are in the EU or UK, you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

**Your Rights:**
- Right to access your data (Section 7.1)
- Right to deletion ("right to be forgotten") — Section 6.2
- Right to data portability — Section 7.1
- Right to object to data processing — Section 7.3
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority

**Our Legal Basis:**
- For providing the Service: **Contract** (your purchase agreement)
- For improving the Service: **Legitimate interest** (improving product quality)
- For legal compliance: **Legal obligation**
- For model training: **Consent** (you can opt-out anytime — Section 7.3)

**Data Protection Officer:**
If you have GDPR-related questions:
- Email privacy@aisprintscompany.com
- We will respond within 30 days

### 9.2 California Users (CCPA)

If you are in California, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

**Your Rights:**
- Right to know what data we collect
- Right to delete personal data
- Right to opt-out of data sales
- Right to non-discrimination

**Our Compliance:**
- We do NOT sell your data to third parties (CCPA "sale" includes sharing for marketing) — so this right is not applicable
- You can still request deletion (Section 6.2)
- You can opt-out of marketing (Section 7.3)

To exercise CCPA rights:
- Email privacy@aisprintscompany.com
- Specify your request (access, deletion, opt-out)
- We will respond within 45 days

## 12. SPECIFIC DATA SCENARIOS

### Scenario A: You Share Mental Health Information

**What happens:**
- Your conversation is stored in our database
- It may be anonymized for model training (unless you opt-out — Section 7.3)
- It is only accessible to our authorized staff and customer support

**Your control:**
- You can request deletion at any time
- You can opt-out of training data use
- You can review all stored conversations by requesting a data export

### Scenario B: You Experience a Crisis

**What happens:**
- If you disclose a mental health crisis, we may:
  - Pause your sprint (communication pauses)
  - Record the incident for legal/compliance purposes
  - Follow the protocol in our Mental Health Disclaimer
- Crisis incident data is stored separately from your main conversation history
- This data may be retained longer for legal documentation

**Your rights:**
- You can request deletion of crisis records after resolution
- You can request a copy of what we documented

### Scenario C: Your Companion Generates Harmful Content

**What happens:**
- If your companion generates inaccurate or harmful advice, we:
  - Log the incident (for model improvement)
  - May reach out to understand the context
  - Do NOT use this specific conversation for model training

**Your rights:**
- You can request deletion of specific conversation sections
- You can ask us not to use this data for improvement purposes
