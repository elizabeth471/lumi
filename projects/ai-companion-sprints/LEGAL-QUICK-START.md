## THE THREE BIGGEST RISKS

### Risk 1: Customer Mental Health Crisis or Self-Harm
**What happens:** Customer discloses crisis language, you escalate, but harm occurs anyway
**Mitigation:** Crisis protocol (detect trigger phrases → provide crisis hotlines → pause service)
**Residual risk:** MODERATE (disclaimers + protocol reduce liability but don't eliminate it)
**Insurance covers:** Legal defense + settlement

### Risk 2: Companion Generates Harmful Advice
**What happens:** Claude says something wrong/harmful, customer follows it, is harmed
**Mitigation:** Clear instructions ("never give medical advice"), strong disclaimers
**Residual risk:** MODERATE (you're responsible for companion's instructions even if Claude misbehaves)
**Insurance covers:** Legal defense + settlement

### Risk 3: Data Breach Exposing Intimate Conversations
**What happens:** Hackers access database, customer's private conversations leaked
**Mitigation:** Encryption, access controls, regular backups
**Residual risk:** LOW-MODERATE (data breaches are rare if you do basic security)
**Insurance covers:** Breach notification, customer credit monitoring, regulatory fines

## THE HONEST RISK ASSESSMENT

### Best Case (Most Likely)
- Zero major incidents in first 30 days
- Few refund requests (handled professionally)
- No legal threats
- Insurance sits idle (doesn't cost you money in claims)

### Realistic Case (Expected at Scale)
- 1–5 crisis incidents detected and escalated
- 10–20 refund requests
- 0–1 legal threat (probably doesn't litigate)
- Insurance pays for legal advice if needed

### Worst Case (Unlikely but Possible)
- Customer suicide/self-harm, family sues
- Legal defense costs: $200K–$500K
- Settlement: $100K–$1M (jurisdiction dependent)
- Insurance covers both
- Regulatory investigation (low probability, manageable if it happens)

**Bottom line:** You can't eliminate risk. But you can manage it. That's why you get insurance.

## WHAT HAPPENS IF THINGS GO WRONG

### Customer Refuses to Accept "Not Therapy" Disclaimer
**You:** Offer full refund, thank them for their interest
**Cost:** $9.99
**Outcome:** They go use a competitor

### Customer in Crisis Receives Hotline, Refuses to Call
**You:** Follow up at 24 hours, offer refund, pause sprint
**Cost:** $9.99 refund + time
**Outcome:** You've done everything right; if they harm themselves, that's not on you

### Customer Sues, Claims Your Service Caused Harm
**You:** Forward to insurance immediately
**Insurance:** Assigns attorney, legal defense included
**Cost:** Covered by insurance (except deductible, typically $5–10K)
**Outcome:** Settlement or dismissal (most likely)

### Data Breach Happens
**You:** Notify customers (required by law)
**Insurance:** Covers notification, credit monitoring, regulatory fines
**Cost:** Covered by cyber insurance
**Outcome:** Reputation damage, but company survives
