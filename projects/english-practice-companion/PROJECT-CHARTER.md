# ENGLISH PRACTICE COMPANION — Project Charter

**Project Name:** English Practice Companion (Working title: "English Texting Coach" or "EnglishPal")

**Status:** Active Development (Spinoff from AI Companion Sprints)

**Owner:** Elizabeth

**Launch Target:** Week 3 (soft launch with 50-100 customers, global targeting)

---

## PROJECT MISSION

Build and launch a 4-week subscription service where non-native English speakers purchase time-bound "sprints" to practice written English with a personalized AI coach. Goal-focused on business English, fluency, interview prep, or casual conversation.

**Core Value Proposition:**
- Personalized English practice coach for 4 weeks on YOUR specific goal
- Time-bound (clear endpoint, reduces dependency)
- Affordable ($9.99/sprint)
- Built on Claude API (understands nuance, accent, context)
- Global addressable market (400M+ non-native English speakers)
- Recurring revenue through chained sprints

**Why Separate Product:**
- Largest addressable market of any companion (English learners >> other languages)
- Different marketing channels (English learning communities vs. dating/motivation)
- Different positioning (career/travel prep vs. emotional support)
- Potential for premium tiers (Business English, IELTS prep, accent coaching)
- Dedicated landing page + marketing focus maximizes capture

---

## SUCCESS CRITERIA (Phase 1)

- [ ] Product documented and fully specified
- [ ] MVP launched (English Coach companion, email delivery, 3 proficiency levels)
- [ ] 100+ customers in first 2 weeks

## PROJECT SCOPE (Phase 1)

**In Scope:**
- 1 English practice companion (scalable across proficiency levels: A1, A2, B1, B2)
- Email-based delivery
- Stripe payment integration
- Simple landing page (English learner-focused messaging)
- Customer onboarding flow
- Operations/quality control process
- Financial modeling + sustainability plan
- Risk mitigation documentation
- Tech stack decisions (same as parent project)

**Out of Scope (Phase 2+):**
- Pronunciation audio feedback
- Mobile app
- Slack/Discord integration
- Business English certifications
- IELTS/TOEFL prep specialization (future premium)
- Multiple languages (English only Phase 1)
