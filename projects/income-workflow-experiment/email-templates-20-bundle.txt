BONUS TIPS FOR EACH TEMPLATE
================================================================================

✓ KEEP IT SHORT: Most should be 50-100 words. Respect their time.

✓ PERSONALIZE: Replace every [bracket] with something specific to them. Generic = delete.

✓ ONE ASK PER EMAIL: Don't ask for 3 things. Pick one.

✓ SUBJECT LINES MATTER: Good subject = they open it. Boring subject = trash.

✓ PROOFREAD: Typos kill credibility. Spell check twice.

✓ SEND EARLY/MID-WEEK: Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11am = best open rates.

✓ FOLLOW UP: 2-3 follow-ups are normal. Giving up after one = leaving money on the table.
